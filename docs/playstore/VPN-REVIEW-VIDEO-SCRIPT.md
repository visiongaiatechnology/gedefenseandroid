# Google Play VpnService Review Video Script

**Release:** `0.27.4-beta.1`

Record one continuous, readable device capture. Do not edit around disclosures or permission prompts.

1. Launch GeDefense from a fresh/reset setup state.
2. Show the onboarding page explaining the local VPN protection model.
3. Open the GeDefense VPN disclosure.
4. Keep the disclosure on screen long enough for the reviewer to read the local-processing, no-TLS-interception and no-traffic-history-upload statements.
5. Tap the explicit acceptance action.
6. Show Android's system VPN consent dialog and approve it.
7. Start protection and show the persistent Android VPN/protection state.
8. Open the Protection/XDR surface so the security purpose is visible.
9. Open Privacy & Data Processing and show that VPN disclosure consent can be revoked.
10. Revoke consent and show that protection stops/refuses restart until disclosure is accepted again.

The video must show actual release behavior. Do not use mockups, debug-only screens or narration that promises functionality the binary does not implement.
