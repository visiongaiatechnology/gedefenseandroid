# Community Conduct

GeDefense welcomes technical disagreement and adversarial review. Critique code, architecture, evidence and claims directly; do not attack people.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of another person's private data, spam and knowingly malicious submissions are not accepted. Security research performed in good faith is welcome when it avoids unnecessary harm and follows the reporting guidance in `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may moderate contributions or participation that materially obstruct safe technical collaboration. Decisions should be tied to observable conduct, not disagreement with a technical position.
